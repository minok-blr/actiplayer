1 · First launch — GuideScreen
FLOWSTATE
Music that rides with you
FlowState watches how you're moving and matches the music's energy — hands in gloves, phone in pocket. Here's the whole setup:
1
Rate your music
In the Library tab, rate tracks by energy: 1 = couch, 5 = send it. A handful of each is enough to start. Unrated tracks play as middle energy.
2
Pair a heart-rate monitor (optional)
In the Session tab, tap Set up monitor — the app will ask to turn on Bluetooth if it's off. On a Garmin watch, enable Broadcast Heart Rate first (Settings → Sensors & Accessories → Wrist Heart Rate). Chest straps just work.
3
Pick your activity, start, pocket the phone
Choose Snowboarding or Cycling on the Session tab (more coming), then tap Start session and ride. Working hard gets your high-energy tracks, easing off calms the music — automatically.
4
Override anytime
CHILL / AUTO / HYPE buttons at the bottom of the session screen. AUTO hands control back to the engine.
Reopen this guide anytime with the ? button at the top.
